My friend Ryoko gave me this traditional Japanese apron, a maekake. It wraps snugly around the hips and provides a little bit of lower back support. 

![](https://images.kenchinpurcell.com/IMG_5251.jpeg)

The traditional color is indigo blue, and the cloth is cotton canvas.

I really wanted to find out how she was able to print just one custom apron, but I can’t find a source in the US. I suspect it was printed in Japan.
