Our trip up until this point had been mostly driven by seeing family, with extra sightseeing bits for us along the way. Now things change bit, this next week is an adventure just for us. We were going to navigate the Llangollen canal in our very own rented narrowboat.

Our first job was to get from Manchester Airport to Chirk, Wales. I chose to go via Transport for Wales (TfW) which connected in Chester. This is a trip across the industrial north of England, and the train station at Chester on a drizzly morning seemed part of that industrial vibe.

![](https://images.kenchinpurcell.com/IDG_20260926_110109_992.jpg)

Chirk is a small town near the Welsh border, and is home to a huge factory that makes Cadbury hot chocolate mix. The marina where we were going to pick up our narrowboat was a mile and a half out of town, but our first job was to walk into town center, get some lunch and buy some supplies for the boat. We wound up at the Teddy Bear Cafe sandwich shop.

![](https://images.kenchinpurcell.com/IMG_3949.jpeg "Help")

I don't know if it holds across the UK but Teddy Bear featured "Coronation Chicken", a lightly curried chicken salad that was to be on most pub menus the coming week.

The helpful people at Black Prince at the marina had told us that the boat's fridge was small, so we only bought a few things at the local Spar and the fruit and veg stand nextdoor. Again, not sure for the whole of the UK but Chirk was replete with meat pies. I picked up a pork pie from the local butcher across the street from Teddy Bear.

Now we just needed to get ourselves plus groceries to the marina. The marina emailed a list of taxi companies and suggested pre-booking. Three weeks before the trip I called a couple of them up. One didn't pick up, the next did but then she just said "Love, call back closer to the date <click>". 

So while in Switzerland I called back and tried to arrange a taxi. Accents were thick and taxi people seem very impatient, at least with Americans.

So now we were on the corner of Teddy Bear and Spar in Chirk and no taxi was showing up. Nervousness set in, we needed to be at the marina by 3:00 pm. I finally called they guy I thought I had booked for 2:00 pm, and he said in a miffed tone that he was there at 12:00 pm and I wasn't, <click>. In theory we both are speaking English here, but in practice...

Thick accents, miscommunication , no taxi. Lucked out on another taxi, made it in time. Thorough tour of the boat, super brief driving lesson, and we were off.

Michele thought the canal starts out narrow, actually a nice wide section. Right into a one way tunnel, then the aqueduct. Pubs, highway. Found a quiet place to park, dinner on board.

Pretty much a yurt in a tube. Twelve-volt lifestyle. Diesel heater, hot water from the engine. Microwave and toaster when the engine is running, but didn’t need those. Stove on propane. Everything else twelve volt, pumps, toilet, lighting.
