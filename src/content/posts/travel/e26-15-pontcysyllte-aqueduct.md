I shouldn't have worried, everything was perfectly chill overnight. The canal police did not come by to kick us out.

We walked our boat forward 50 yards, tied off and filled our water tank. Then it was off over River Dee on the Pontcysyllte aqueduct.

Watered up, then three boat flotilla across the aqueduct. Sharp turn into the Llangollen canal, turning boats, confusing. 

Beautiful miles. One lane last stretch, Peter and Margaret from Ellesmere. 

Moored in the basin. Texas. Checked out town, got a pasty and groceries. 

Horseshoe falls, chain bridge.
