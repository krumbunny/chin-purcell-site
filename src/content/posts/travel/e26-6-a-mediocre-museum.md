In the morning we visited Lund’s oldest store with Evalena, then collected our bags and headed out. We spent the afternoon in Copenhagen, because our plane to Geneva wasn’t until the evening. It was a Monday, so many things were closed, but we decided to try the Nationalmuseet, since it was close to the train station and, well, was open.

I’m a sucker for museums and am usually easy to please. I thought the National Museum had good stuff but was confusingly laid out. It’s not at all clear how to even access the exhibits. The entrance area was a three-floor atrium lined with mostly blank doors.
￼

Most doors were closed. I went back to the ticket counter and they advised to go up a floor, pick a door, turn the handle and push hard. Which is not something that I would do at your average museum, or anywhere not my house, but it worked! I would like to reiterate that these doors are unlabeled, with very stiff door closers. Maybe the Danes just know what to do.

I learned that there’s a lot of gold and silver out there in the Danish bogs.

That evening we were herded onto an EasyJet flight to Geneva. Moo.
