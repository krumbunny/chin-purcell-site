The night before we stayed at the Ibis near Geneva airport, which is a 15 minute walk. The first part, through the train station then along the convention center, isn’t too bad, but then you come to a two story high flight of stairs leading to a bridge over a highway, with the hotel on the other side of the bridge. Hauling your luggage up the stairs is daunting, but there is an elevator under the stairs. To use the elevator you have open a door and enter into a small pitch black room and then fumble around for the up button. Until the elevator door opens and throws some light into the situation it’s a spooky experience.

Vevey was lovely as always.
￼

￼

￼

Wednesday was our day to explore. We took the MOB mountain train towards Gstaad and got off at an “on demand” stop at La Tine. It’s just like a bus, you press a button and the train stops at a very rural siding in a mountain valley.
￼

We had a lovely hike up the valley to Châteaux d’Oex. About 20 years ago we had gone to Châteaux d’Oex as a family to go skiing, but we decided to try luge instead. There was tons of snow and we all took some crazy spills but had a great time.
￼

Sadly Châteaux d’Oex doesn’t get much snow anymore and the ski slope closed several years ago. The town is still pretty though. Michele and I stopped for a crepe before doing a loop around Châteaux d’Oex and then taking the train back.
￼

￼

￼

￼

Another favorite of ours that has closed is Ze Fork down on the Quai. It has been replaced with a more beef focused restaurant. We ate instead at a restaurant near the market square, which was highly rated on Google and recommended by some AI that I used but I would say wasn’t really what we were after. I’m getting annoyed by online reviews, and the AI opinions built on them. I think I need to focus more on sizing things up in person.
